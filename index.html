✨ What This App Does For You

Some AI assistants are brilliant writers and problem solvers, but they cannot see. If you paste a screenshot into a conversation, they have no idea what is on it. GLM-5.3-Full-Vision-Bridge fixes exactly that problem.

The app sits quietly on your own computer and acts as a translator. When an image arrives, it looks at the picture using a vision model that runs locally through Ollama, writes a careful description of what it sees, and hands that description back to your text-only assistant. From your assistant's point of view, the screenshot simply became readable text.

Everything happens on your machine. Your screenshots are not shipped off to a stranger's server. The bridge speaks the same language as popular AI tools, which is why it works smoothly with ZCode and anything else that expects an OpenAI-style connection.

🧠 How It Works, In Plain English

Imagine you are travelling and cannot read the local language, but you have a friend who can. You show the friend a sign, the friend tells you what it says, and you carry on with your conversation. The bridge is that friend.

Three moving parts are involved. First, your text-only assistant, which is very good at thinking but blind. Second, a vision model running on your own computer through Ollama, which can actually look at pictures. Third, the bridge itself, which passes messages between the two in a format both sides already understand.

Because the bridge presents a familiar OpenAI-compatible interface, tools like ZCode do not need any special plugin or patch. They send an image as they always would, the bridge quietly converts it into a written description, and the assistant replies as if it had seen the picture all along.

💻 What Your Computer Needs

A comfortably ordinary modern Windows laptop or desktop is enough. For the smoothest experience, aim for the following:

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit.
  • Roughly 8 GB of memory, with 16 GB giving noticeably smoother results.
  • A few gigabytes of free disk space for the vision model files.
  • Ollama installed and running, since that is what powers the image reading.
  • A stable internet connection only for the initial downloads.

If your machine is older or has less memory, the app still works, but describing large or very detailed screenshots may take a little longer.

🖱️ Working With ZCode

ZCode is where most people will use this bridge, and the pairing is deliberately simple. The bridge publishes a normal, OpenAI-compatible address on your computer. You give that address to ZCode once, and from then on images just work.

A typical first setup looks like this: open the bridge, note the local address and model name it reports, open ZCode's settings, choose a custom or OpenAI-compatible provider, paste the address, and save. After that, every screenshot you drop into a chat gets described automatically before your assistant answers.

If you ever move the bridge to a different port, remember to update the address in ZCode as well. The two need to be pointing at the same place.

🔧 If Something Does Not Work

Most hiccups come from one of two places: Ollama not running, or the address in your AI tool not matching the bridge. Check those first and you will solve the majority of problems in under a minute.

  • Nothing happens when I paste an image. Make sure the bridge window is still open and that Ollama is active in the background.
  • The reply ignores my screenshot. The address saved in ZCode probably does not match the one the bridge is showing. Copy it again carefully.
  • It is slow. Vision models are heavier than text models. Closing other demanding programs usually helps.
  • The description is vague. Very small or blurry screenshots are hard for any model. A clean, full-size screenshot gives much better results.
  • Windows blocked the start. Use More info then Run anyway, or start the app as administrator.

Still stuck? The releases page lists each version along with its notes, which often mention fixes for exactly the issue you are seeing.
